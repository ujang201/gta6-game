SOLMAR BAY

Five jobs. One crew. One sunset city.

Leo Marin came to Solmar Bay to drive — just drive. But the Marlin Crew pays better than any taxi stand, and Rosa the fixer has five jobs with his name on them. Steal the cars. Lose the cops. Take the money and run.

How to play

  • WASD — walk / drive   Mouse — look around (drag or click to lock)
  • Shift — run   E — steal / enter a car, E again to step out
  • Space — handbrake (driving) / shoot (on foot)   Click — shoot
  • F — punch   M — start the next job   P / Esc — pause
  • Drive into a blue Pay-N-Spray bay to lose the cops ($100). Rest at the green safehouse to heal.
